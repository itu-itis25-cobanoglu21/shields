# SHIELDS Hat Cosmetics Set 01 polish

## Faceting and shading

The original crowns, broad brims, and Bandana folds were flat-shaded. The approved shapes already had 36–72 segments around their visible curves. Flat normals made adjacent polygons catch the light independently, and large highlight swatch blocks added abrupt color steps on the crowns.

- **Tavern Cap.** Smoothed the folded crown and the visible lower band. The band edges and cross stitches still read separately.
- **Rogue Gambler.** Smoothed the felt crown, swept brim, and Oxblood band around their curves. Kept the brim rim, band edges, and card crisp.
- **Tricorn Scout.** Smoothed the felt crown and cocked brim surfaces. Smoothed the binding along its length while keeping its bevel boundaries sharp.
- **Dice Bandana.** Smoothed the fitted crown, broad wrap faces, knot lobes, and tails. Kept the layered fold edges and patch detail readable.

The pass uses face smoothing with explicit sharp edges at structural rings and across steep normal changes. Angle thresholds range from 27° to 54° by part. Large crown highlights use the existing base swatch so the updated normals can carry the gradient. Weighted normals and subdivision were unnecessary. The three approved hats retain their exact vertex positions, proportions, and topology.

## Dice Bandana

The crown peak rose from 211.0 to 226.6 mm above the shared HeadCosmeticAnchor. The upper crown grew outward by up to 11.3 mm, blended from the brow line and gathered edge. The crown and wrap folds share the fuller shape, with a slight asymmetry toward the back. The knot and tails retain their positions.

The original four-pip patch moved 8.2 mm upward and was re-seated on the wrap. Its original height and pip arrangement were retained. The cloth shape provided enough volume for the intended look, so the outfit uses no separate hair mesh.

The original and polished hats were compared from four angles. The Bandana close-up uses matching cameras and lighting to show the added crown volume and the patch and knot placement. Blender checks found no intersections with any of the 22 available eye, eyebrow, and mouth variants. The Bandana's visible crown and wrap faces clear the head. Its inner lining still seats into the skull.
